Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Committed to transparent, secure data handling across all group platforms.

1. Information We Collect

When you voluntarily interact with Navdiva Group websites through our corporate contact enquiry or career application forms, we collect information you supply including your name, email address, contact phone number, company name, department interest, and application background.

2. How We Use Submitted Information

We process submitted data exclusively to evaluate business partnerships, fulfill customer inquiries, screen candidate resumes, and coordinate with you regarding relevant Navdiva Group initiatives. We do not sell, license, or barter your personal information to third-party ad brokers.

3. Security & Cloud Access Control

All sensitive operational submissions are securely stored in our cloud infrastructure managed under Firebase Firestore security rules and authenticated administrator credential gates. Only authorized company personnel have access to review incoming messages.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain business inquiries and applicant records only for as long as necessary to satisfy legal compliance, accounting, or operational recruitment needs. You may request deletion of your submitted details at any time by contacting our privacy officer.

5. Contact Our Privacy Office

For inquiries regarding this privacy statement or our digital practices, email us at corporate@navdiva.com.
