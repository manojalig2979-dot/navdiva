Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

1. Introduction

Welcome to Navdiva Group Pvt Ltd. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy sets out how we collect, use, process and protect any information that you give us when you use this website and our affiliated services (including NDTechHub, NDMart, and Rameshta).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name and job title
  • Contact information including email address and phone number
  • Demographic information such as postcode, preferences and interests
  • Website usage data and analytics
  • Employment application data (if you apply via our Careers page)

3. How We Use the Information

We require this information to understand your needs and provide you with a better service, and in particular for the following reasons:

  • Internal record keeping.
  • To improve our products and services across our business units.
  • We may periodically send promotional emails about new products, special offers or other information.
  • From time to time, we may also use your information to contact you for market research purposes.

4. Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. In order to prevent unauthorised access or disclosure, we have put in place suitable physical, electronic and managerial procedures to safeguard and secure the information we collect online. Specifically, we utilize secure cloud infrastructure and encrypted database connections for all sensitive information.

5. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to other websites of interest (e.g., our subsidiary domains like NDTechHub.com). However, once you have used these links to leave our site, you should note that we do not have any control over that other website. Therefore, we cannot be responsible for the protection and privacy of any information which you provide whilst visiting such sites.

6. Contact Us

If you believe that any information we are holding on you is incorrect or incomplete, or if you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please write to or email us as soon as possible at info@navdiva.com.
