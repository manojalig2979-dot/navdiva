Bringing precision engineering principles into digital entrepreneurship, he established Navdiva Group as an umbrella for specialized ventures in software, retail, culture, and infrastructure.

Under his direction, the group prioritizes fast iteration, zero-waste operations, and long-term customer relationships over speculative growth.
