Our Journey: From AMU to the ND Group

Phase 1

The Foundation (Aligarh)

The story of the ND Group begins in Aligarh, where our founder, Manoj, pursued his passion for building things. After completing his B.Tech and M.Tech in Civil Engineering from Aligarh Muslim University (AMU), he developed a deep understanding of structural integrity and technical precision—skills that would later become the backbone of his business ventures.

Phase 2

The Vision (Delhi)

With a master’s degree and a drive to excel, Manoj moved to Delhi. After gaining valuable experience serving various reputed companies, he realized that his true calling was to create something of his own. He saw a gap in how technology and services were being delivered to common people and decided to bridge it.

Phase 3

The Birth of NAVDIVA

In the heart of India's capital, NAVDIVA was born. What started as a single company quickly evolved into the NAVDIVA Group (The ND Group) as Manoj’s vision expanded. He didn't just want to build one business; he wanted to create an ecosystem of solutions—ranging from tech services (NDTechHub) to spiritual platforms (Rameshta) and now, a premium shopping experience.

Where We Are Today

Today, the ND Group stands as a testament to that journey. Based in Delhi but rooted in the values of Aligarh, we combine engineering-level precision with a customer-first approach. Whether you are looking for household essentials or specialized products, you are supported by the decades of expertise and the trusted name of the ND Group.
